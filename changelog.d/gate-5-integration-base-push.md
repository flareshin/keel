### Changed

- ship-gate asks for an owner approval (`keel approve <root_run_id>`) on a `git push` that may land on the integration base, such as `git push origin feature:main`, `git push origin main`, or a bare or colon-less `git push` under `push.default=upstream` whose upstream is `main`, even where `push_approval` is not set: such a push is a merge. A destination it cannot read (a remote with `remote.<name>.push`, `push.default=matching`, a detached HEAD, a run-time word) counts as the integration base. `keel evidence check` reads the same destination rule, so it reports the missing approval on `main` after an unapproved local merge. Repos with `auto_merge=true` still land on PASS plus tests. A push of `main` after an approved local merge passes.
