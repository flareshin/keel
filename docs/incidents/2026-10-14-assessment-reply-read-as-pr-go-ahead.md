# 2026-10-14: a short go-ahead on an assessment started a PR, a push and reviews

- Rule: risk.
- Event: the owner asked for a summary and an assessment of where their goals stood. After the assessment, a short "go ahead" reply was read as approval for the whole to-do list in it: the agent opened a PR, pushed a branch and started lens-review workflows. The owner stopped it as overkill; they had asked for an assessment only.
- Cost if unblocked: an unrequested PR and push reach other people and cannot be taken back quietly, and the review runs spent tokens on work nobody asked for.
- Why the rule: a reply is not delivery consent. PR creation, drafts included, needs the owner's `keel approve` for the current candidate and an explicit `/ship1`; the row took that boundary on 2026-10-16.
