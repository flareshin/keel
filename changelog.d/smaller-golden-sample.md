### Changed

- The default replay of the ship-gate and hook golden snapshots is smaller: every `extra` ship-gate case and every adversarial hook payload that is not a wrong-type payload used to be kept whole, and each is now sampled like the other groups, keeping the first case of each outcome class in its group (the ship-gate `extra` cases per test, the hook payloads as one group). The ship-gate sample drops from 995 to 544 of 1757 recorded cases and the hook sample from 549 to 325 of 1069, a bit under half the replay time, and every outcome class of the full corpus is still replayed. `SHIP_GATE_PARITY_FULL=1` and `HOOK_PARITY_FULL=1` still replay every case.
